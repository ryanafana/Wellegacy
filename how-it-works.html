How It Works

Questions

Things People Ask Us

Do I have to apply to get a quote or see pricing? +
Yes — and intentionally. Wellegacy is not a storefront with published packages. Every itinerary is custom, which means pricing depends entirely on where you want to go, when, with whom, and what experience level you're seeking. The application is how we scope your journey accurately. Investment ranges are on the Investment Guide page so you can assess fit before applying.
Is there a fee just for the consultation? +
The initial discovery call is complimentary. If we move forward together, a planning retainer is applied — and credited toward your overall investment. The purpose of the call is mutual: we want to confirm this is the right fit for both of us before either of us commits.
Do you book flights? +
Wellegacy can advise on flight strategy — timing, class of service, routing, and points/miles maximization — but flights are generally booked independently by clients. This keeps you in full control of your loyalty programs. We walk you through exactly what to book and when.
What makes this different from a travel agent? +
The depth of curation and the relationship. A traditional travel agent books what you ask for. Wellegacy asks better questions first — about your chapter, your energy, what restoration looks like right now — and builds something that fits the woman you actually are. LaTanya is also a credentialed wellness professional, which shapes how rest and intention are woven into the journey, not just added as an afterthought.
I've never traveled solo. Is this for me? +
Absolutely — and you may be exactly who this was built for. A curated solo itinerary with concierge support is often the safest and most expansive first solo trip a woman can take. You are never alone in the logistical sense. You're free in every other sense.
How far in advance should I apply? +
For peak season or milestone group trips, we recommend applying 4–6 months in advance. For flexible timing or off-peak destinations, 8–12 weeks can work. Rush curation is possible in some cases — mention your timeline in the application and we'll be direct about what's feasible.
What if my plans change after booking? +
Life moves. Wellegacy's approach includes building flexibility into your itinerary where possible and advising on travel protection that covers real-life circumstances. We walk you through vendor-specific change and cancellation policies before anything is booked — no surprises.

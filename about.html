The Origin

She wasn't running from burnout. She was running toward something she didn't have a word for yet.

LaTanya spent years doing the demanding, invisible work that high-achieving women know intimately. She managed multimillion-dollar contracts with precision and grace. She showed up fully in rooms that rarely returned the favor. She was decorated. Credentialed. Respected. And running on empty in a way she had no language for yet.

The trip that changed everything wasn't the most expensive one she'd ever taken. It was the quality of attention behind it. Someone asked the right questions first. Someone curated around who she was arriving as and who she was trying to become. That experience didn't just restore her. It recalibrated something deep. She came home different.

She built Wellegacy from that place. Not as a travel agency. As a deliberate response to a specific kind of woman: the one who has spent years planning everything for everyone else and keeps quietly postponing herself.

She brought her whole self to it. The M.A. in Health and Wellness Coaching. The ASTA affiliation. The contracting officer's instinct for precision and accountability. The partnerships with premium to luxury land and sea providers. The technical rigor. And more than any credential on paper, the embodied knowledge of what it feels like to arrive somewhere and finally be held by the itinerary instead of managing it.

That is what she builds for you.

The Credentials

What LaTanya brings to every itinerary

01

M.A. Health & Wellness Coaching (HWC)

This is not a certification picked up between trips. The M.A. HWC designation means every itinerary is built with genuine working knowledge of how restoration actually functions, physiologically, psychologically, and personally. The trip is designed around the whole woman. Not just the wish list.

02

ASTA-Affiliated Travel Professional

ASTA membership holds travel professionals to an enforced code of ethics and professional standards. For you, that means Wellegacy is accountable to something beyond its own word. Your investment is protected by a standard with real teeth behind it.

03

Virgin Voyages Partner

LaTanya's partnership with Virgin Voyages is not incidental. She chose it because she has experienced it. These ships are built for adults who want full-sensory luxury without the noise of a floating theme park. She knows how to place the right woman on the right sailing and make it feel inevitable.

04

DAU-Certified Contracting Officer

Managing multimillion-dollar government contracts requires a level of precision and accountability that does not clock out. LaTanya carries that same discipline into every vendor negotiation, itinerary draft, and client timeline. Nothing is assumed. Nothing falls through.

05

Premium to Luxury Land and Sea Partnerships

Wellegacy is partnered with a curated portfolio of premium to luxury land and sea vendors across destinations worldwide. These are not directory listings. They are vetted relationships with providers who understand the Wellegacy standard and what it means to serve a woman who has earned this trip.

06

Lived Experience of Restoration

No credential covers this one. LaTanya has been the woman in the airport, running on fumes, telling herself she's fine. She has been the woman who arrived at the destination and didn't know how to stop performing. She builds from the inside of that experience, because she knows exactly what it feels like when the right trip finally finds you.

What Wellegacy Stands For

The principles underneath every itinerary

I.

Intention Before Everything

No itinerary is built until LaTanya understands who you are, what you're carrying, and what you're reaching for. The planning is only as good as the listening that precedes it.

II.

Luxury Without Performance

Wellegacy's idea of luxury is not the most expensive room. It is the one that actually restores you. Quality over spectacle. Presence over optics. The trip that makes you feel like yourself again.

III.

Precision as an Act of Care

Details matter because your time matters. A missed transfer, a wrong room, a vendor who does not know you are coming. These are not minor inconveniences. They are failures of care. Wellegacy treats precision as a love language.

IV.

This Work Is Personal

LaTanya doesn't curate from the outside in. She has needed this, sought this, and built this from the inside of a life that required it. This service exists because she knows, firsthand, what's at stake when a woman finally decides to receive.
